# 15.0.0: ten local coaches, Pocket first, Turbo as the fallback

Status: planned 2026-10-26, not started. A major release: it changes how local coaches work and removes the
local designer, so `me/` needs a migration.

## The decision

- **Local coaches are a fixed set of ten, made by the maintainer.** Coach Andy plus four men and five women.
  Listeners can't design or bring a local coach any more; every custom voice is a web coach.
- **Four web coaches stay as they are** (Reed on Fish, Ellis on ElevenLabs, Nolan on Gemini, Owen on Inworld).
  A listener with a service key keeps auditions, custom voices and faster builds, as today.
- **Pocket TTS speaks local coaches by default; Chatterbox Turbo is the fallback.** On every computer: Pocket on
  the Mac's processor, on an NVIDIA card, or on a PC's processor.
- **Qwen3 leaves the shipped engine entirely**: the qwen3 speaking engine and the local designer (Qwen3-TTS
  VoiceDesign) with `coaches design` and the local `coaches keep`. A designer stays only as a maintainer tool
  for making the ten coaches (Phase A).
- **No Hugging Face login in any user flow** (most users aren't technical). Users download only the public
  `kyutai/pocket-tts-without-voice-cloning` model. The gated `kyutai/pocket-tts` is used once, by the
  maintainer, to make each coach's voice file.

The evidence is in `docs/audio-bench/results/2026-10-26-{mac,pc,pc-nvidia}/`. Pocket had the fewest failing
takes and the lowest word error rate, and it was the fastest everywhere:

| | Pocket | Turbo |
|---|---|---|
| Mac, processor | 0.154 render ratio, 0.7% WER | 0.396 (MLX), 1.1% |
| PC, RTX 2080 Super | 0.293, 0.4%, 1 of 88 takes failing | 0.529, 1.3%, 6 of 88 failing |
| PC, processor | 0.427, 0.7% | 2.497, 0.9% |

## How a coach's voice works (why two files)

```
description ──► designer (Qwen3 VoiceDesign or VoxCPM2, maintainer only) ──► clip.wav   (the master)
                                                                                 │
                              ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────┐
                              ▼                                                              ▼
            Turbo: analyses clip.wav itself on the user's machine         Pocket: the gated model turns clip.wav
            (public, a second or two per build; first 15 s for the        into its voice state ONCE, on the
            prompt tokens, first 10 s for the decoder)                    maintainer's PC ─► pocket.safetensors (shipped)
```

- The public Pocket refuses an audio file (`has_voice_cloning` is False) but loads a saved `.safetensors`
  state; tested 2026-10-26: no token, an empty HF cache, and output identical to the gated model's render
  from the clip (same seed, differences only at rounding level).
- `clip.wav` is the source of truth. Any engine's voice data can be remade from it. `pocket.safetensors` is
  tied to the Pocket model it was made with (pin `pocket-tts==4.0.1` and the `english_2026-10` config: gated
  weights `db04f0e…`, public weights `a7056f5…`). A new Pocket model means re-exporting all ten.
- Don't ship Turbo's saved form (`conds.pt`): Turbo's analysis is deterministic and cheap, a pickle is unsafe
  to load, and MLX and PyTorch would each need their own.

## Each coach ships

```
coaches/coach-<name>-local/         # see decision D1 for where this lives
  persona.md          schema 6: name, description, [voice] (the design description), [design] (record), [local]
  clip.wav            the master: 24 kHz mono 16-bit, 18 to 22 s
  clip.txt            its exact words
  pocket.safetensors  Pocket's voice state, about 6.4 MB, full precision
  pace.json           measured once per engine and model (Pocket; Turbo on PyTorch; Turbo on MLX)
  sample.mp3          about 15 s on Pocket, for setup to play
```

## Decisions to settle at the start

- **D1. Where shipped coaches live. Recommended: in the engine, `coaches/` at the repo root**, not copied
  into `me/personas/`. They're public, versioned assets: a release that improves a coach should reach every
  listener, and ten coaches (about 75 MB) don't belong in each listener's private backup. `me/personas/` keeps
  web coaches, archived coaches and per-computer state; this computer's measured render speed goes in
  `me/personas/<slug>/pace.json` or a `.cache` file, with the shipped `pace.json` as the seed (see
  `voices.seed_pace`). `personas.all()` merges the two places, a `me/` folder winning on a name clash. This
  reverses the 9.x-era move of Andy into `me.example/`, so record why in the changelog.
- **D2. Andy: redesign (recommended) or keep his 10.4 s clip.** His current clip came from the Mac's Qwen3
  designer, and a longer take of the same voice can't be made. Redesign him from his description so all ten
  match in length and quality, then compare old and new by ear. Keep `aliases = ["Coach Demo"]`.
- **D3. The take check's recognizer.** The fallback needs a take check on every computer. Today it runs only
  on a Mac (Parakeet through MLX, `voices.CHECK_MODEL`). Recommended: one recognizer everywhere, the bench's
  Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 through `onnx-asr`, retiring the MLX one. Measure its speed on the PC's processor
  first; if it's too slow, keep MLX on the Mac and use ONNX only on PCs.

## Phase A: make the ten voices (maintainer only, on the RTX 2080 Super PC)

What's already on that PC (2026-10-26):
- Hugging Face login with accepted Pocket terms (`uvx hf auth whoami` works; `auth_check("kyutai/pocket-tts")`
  passes).
- `models/` holds `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign` (rev `5ecdb673…`, 4.3 GB) and `openbmb/VoxCPM2`
  (rev `32279eff…`, 4.7 GB), downloaded with `HF_HUB_CACHE=models`.
- Environments: `.cache/bench/envs/qwen3-cuda/` (qwen-tts 0.1.1, torch 2.6.0 cu124),
  `.cache/design/envs/voxcpm/` (voxcpm 2.0.3, torch 2.8.0 cu128; CUDA confirmed), and
  `.cache/bench/envs/pocket-cuda/` and `pocket-cpu/` (pocket-tts 4.0.1). All git-ignored.

Steps:
1. **Maintainer tools**, checked in but not part of any listener command: `tools/coachmaker/` with
   `design.py` (render takes from a description with either designer, in its own environment like
   `bench_worker.py`), `export_pocket.py` (gated Pocket: `TTSModel.load_model()`,
   `get_state_for_audio_prompt(clip)`, `export_model_state(state, "pocket.safetensors")`), and `qa.py`
   (step 6). Record every coach's description, designer, revision, seed and chosen take in
   `coaches/roster.toml`, so any coach can be remade. Takes go in `.cache/design/` (ignored).
   Check each designer's API against its installed package before writing: qwen-tts's voice-design call
   (likely `generate_voice_design(text, language, instruct)`) and VoxCPM2's (a parenthesized description
   before the text, `cfg_value` about 2.0, `inference_timesteps` about 10).
2. **Bake-off.** Design Andy, Claire and Callum (a low male voice, a calm female voice, an accent: the hard
   cases) on both designers, 3 takes each, same script. The maintainer listens and picks the designer. Note
   VoxCPM2 renders at 48 kHz, so resample to 24 kHz for the clip.
3. **Design the rest** with the winner: 3 to 4 takes per coach. Clip spec: 18 to 22 s; the first 10 s
   carries the voice at its most typical (Turbo's decoder hears only those); no room sound, music or noise;
   ends trimmed to about 0.1 s; level matched across all ten (about -24 dBFS speech, peak under -1 dBFS, the
   same leveling `render.level` uses). Check `clip.txt` against a transcript of the take, not the script, in
   case the designer changed a word.
4. **Pick a take per coach** (maintainer listens). Make Pocket's file with `export_pocket.py`.
5. **Measure pace** for each coach with `knowledge/smoke-test.draft.txt`: Pocket, and Turbo on PyTorch here;
   Turbo on MLX needs one short run on the Mac (or ship it estimated, and the first Mac build measures it).
   Pocket's speech rate is the same on processor and card (same weights), so one key.
6. **QA each coach** (`qa.py`, reusing the bench's script, worker and scoring): the 44 lines, one seed, on
   Pocket (from `pocket.safetensors`, through the public model) and on Turbo (from `clip.wav`). Pass: no
   missed cue, no audio problems, WER under about 1.5% on each engine. Also check that the two engines sound
   like the same person (speaker-embedding similarity of Pocket's and Turbo's takes, e.g. Chatterbox's
   `VoiceEncoder`), and that no two coaches sound alike (pairwise similarity across the ten). A coach that
   fails is redesigned.
7. **Samples**: render `sample.mp3` for each on Pocket.

## Phase B: the engine

Pocket as an engine:
- `pyproject.toml`: add `pocket-tts==4.0.1` to every local extra. Mac: it brings PyTorch for the processor
  next to MLX. PC: it shares chatterbox-tts's torch 2.6.0 (the bench ran pocket-tts on torch 2.6.0 cu124
  and cpu). Run `uv lock` and check both platforms resolve.
- `tools/voices.py`: `ENGINES = ("pocket", "chatterbox")`; add the public Pocket model to `LOCAL_MODELS` with
  its pinned revision (and the tokenizer repo its config names) and to `SIZES_GB`; drop `VOICE_DESIGN`,
  `DESIGN_LINE` and qwen3. Pocket loads its weights through `hf://` paths, so check they land in `models/`
  (`paths.py` points `HF_HUB_CACHE` there) and pass `language="english_2026-10"` explicitly, so a pocket-tts
  default change can't swap the model under the shipped voice files.
- `tools/engines.py`: a `_pocket_speaker(voice)` that loads `TTSModel.load_model(language=…)`, moves to CUDA
  when `cuda_available()`, loads `pocket.safetensors` with `get_state_for_audio_prompt`, and speaks with
  `torch.manual_seed(seed)` then `generate_audio(state, text)`; `runtime()` reports `pocket-tts <v> torch <v>
  cpu|cuda`. Remove the qwen3 MLX path (the `ref_text` branch) and `design_takes`. Keep `release()` freeing
  both engines.
- `tools/personas.py`: `LOCAL_ENGINES = ("pocket", "chatterbox")`; a `pocket_voice` property; `voice_for()`
  builds the Pocket voice dict and, for the fallback, the Turbo one from the same coach.
- `tools/models.py`: `pinned()` loses the designer, gains Pocket (and the ONNX recognizer, per D3).
- `tools/setup.sh` and `tools/doctor.*`: local voices now run on every Mac and PC (Pocket on the processor
  is fast enough), so no "experimental on the processor" warning for Pocket. Download Pocket, Turbo and the
  recognizer at setup, so a fallback never stalls mid-build on a download. Update the sizes in the comments.

The fallback, in `tools/render.py`:
- **Per line**: Pocket gets up to 3 takes (its seed, then the next ones). If none passes the take check,
  Turbo voices the line from the coach's clip, up to 2 takes. The best of all the takes stands. Load Turbo
  lazily, on the first line that needs it; free it at the end.
- **Whole build**: if Pocket can't load (a missing or incompatible voice file, an import error), the build
  runs on Turbo and says so in one line.
- Record which lines fell back, and why, in the build record (`<name>.build.json`, `builds.jsonl`).
- The clip cache key already carries the engine and runtime; make sure a fallback line is cached so a
  rebuild reuses it instead of re-running Pocket's failing takes.
- The lint predicts length from Pocket's pace. A Turbo line runs a little longer or shorter; check that the
  layout uses each clip's real length so the total still lands within 3%.
- Take check on every computer, per D3: `engines.listener()` uses the chosen recognizer; `CHECK_TAKES`
  becomes the 3 + 2 above.

Removing local custom coaches:
- `tools/coaches.py`: remove `design`, `can_design`, `_design_takes`, the local `keep` (a take or a brought
  clip), `set … local.engine`, and the local half of `download` (setup does it). Keep `list`, `last`,
  `measured`, `archive`, `set` for web settings, and every web command (audition, keep, make,
  forget-audition). The `--help` text loses the local-design examples.
- `tools/personas.py`, `tools/check.py`, `me.example/.gitignore`: drop `takes/`.
- `PERSONA_SCHEMA = 6` in `tools/context.py`, with a step in `tools/migrate.py`: move every local persona in
  `me/personas/` that isn't one of the shipped ten into `personas/.archive/` (still found by name for old log
  lines), delete `me/personas/coach-andy-local/` if D1 moves Andy into the engine (keep its pace data for the
  same clip key, if any), and bump schema in the rest.
- `tools/bench.py` and `bench_worker.py` stay as they are (a measurement tool; qwen3 stays a bench candidate).

## Risks and checks

- **Pocket "can't pause"** (2026-10-25 research): the renderer adds the gaps between clips, so it mostly
  doesn't matter, but listen for rushed lines within a paragraph and check the lint's ellipsis rule.
- **Voice files and Pocket upgrades**: pin `pocket-tts` and the language config; a test should fail if the
  pin moves without re-exported voice files.
- **Same person on both engines**: if a coach's Turbo fallback sounds noticeably different, a fallback line
  will stand out. Phase A's similarity check catches it; prefer redesigning the clip over shipping a mismatch.
- **Designer licenses**: Qwen3-TTS and VoxCPM2 are both Apache 2.0; the designed voices are original (no real
  person), which also satisfies Pocket's terms against cloning without consent.
- **Repository size**: about 75 MB for the ten coaches, which is fine (decided). If git history gets heavy
  later, move the voice files to release assets that setup downloads.

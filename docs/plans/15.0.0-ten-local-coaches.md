# 15.0.0: ten local coaches, all on Pocket TTS

Status: planned 2026-10-26; Phase A in progress (four clips kept, six to redesign). Revised the same day, after
QA of the first ten clips: Pocket alone, no Turbo fallback. A major release: it changes how local coaches work
and removes the local designer, so `me/` needs a migration.

## The decision

- **Local coaches are a fixed set of ten, made by the maintainer.** Coach Andy plus four men and five women.
  Listeners can't design or bring a local coach any more; every custom voice is a web coach.
- **Four web coaches stay as they are** (Reed on Fish, Ellis on ElevenLabs, Nolan on Gemini, Owen on Inworld).
  A listener with a service key keeps auditions, custom voices and faster builds, as today.
- **Pocket TTS is the one local engine,** on every computer: the Mac's processor, an NVIDIA card, or a PC's
  processor. Chatterbox Turbo and Qwen3 leave the shipped engine (both stay audio bench candidates). The local
  designer (Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign, `coaches design`, the local `coaches keep`) goes too; a designer stays only
  as a maintainer tool for making the ten (Phase A).
- **No Hugging Face login in any user flow** (most users aren't technical). Users download only the public
  `kyutai/pocket-tts-without-voice-cloning` model. The gated `kyutai/pocket-tts` is used once, by the
  maintainer, to make each coach's voice file.
- **One recognizer for the take check everywhere:** Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 through ONNX, the audio bench's.

### Why Pocket alone

The audio bench (`docs/audio-bench/results/2026-10-26-{mac,pc,pc-nvidia}/`, Coach Andy's 10.4 s clip):

| | Pocket | Turbo |
|---|---|---|
| Mac, processor | 0.154 render ratio, 0.7% WER, 1.6 GB | 0.396 (MLX), 1.1%, 4.7 GB |
| PC, RTX 2080 Super | 0.293, 0.4%, 0.7 GB | 0.529, 1.3%, 3.5 GB |
| PC, processor | 0.427, 0.7%, 1.2 GB | 2.497, 0.9%, 6.5 GB |

Pocket never dropped a cue or made an audio problem in 176 takes; its failing takes were all the recognizer
(a name, "£2,000" for "two thousand pounds", "weight" for "weights"). Turbo's were real: "Blast rep" for "Last
rep" (a dropped cue), "Set your feet, set your feet. Escape the chest" (a runaway), "Part the bell". A Turbo
fallback would have caught failures only Turbo makes, at the cost of a second engine, a same-person check, a
second pace per coach and twice the setup. Without it, a line that fails all of Pocket's takes stops the
build and is named, as today.

### What QA of the first ten clips showed (2026-10-26, Mac, Pocket on the processor, public model)

The 44 bench lines per coach, seed 42, then seeds 43 to 45 for a failing line (`tools/coachmaker/qa.py`):
- **WER 0.4% to 0.9%** for nine coaches, 1.3% for Sam. Length and pace of the clip didn't predict errors:
  Marcus (12.8 s at 249 wpm) was among the cleanest, Claire (21.6 s at 147 wpm) left 1.8 s gaps on first takes.
- **What failed every take: one-word clips.** "Begin." (Maya) and "Hold." (Sam); most first-take failures were
  "Go.", "Begin." and "Hold." too, while "Other way." never failed. Fixed in the script, not the voice: the
  lint now refuses a clip of one word (`tts_build.MIN_CLIP_WORDS`), and `knowledge/tts-rules.md` says why.
- **Pocket copies the clip's pace, a little faster:** Marcus's clip at 249 wpm spoke the workout at 250, Maya
  236 at 238, Callum 197 at 224; Claire 147 at 164, Helen 150 at 168. So the clip's pace is the coach's, and
  five clips are too fast to coach with.
- **The recognizer matters:** the 110M Parakeet the first QA used heard about twice as many lines wrong as the
  0.6B, and reported cues dropped that were said. QA and `design.py` now use the 0.6B (`coachmaker/asr.py`).

## How a coach's voice works

```
description ──► VoxCPM2 (maintainer only, on the PC) ──► clip.wav (the master) ──► gated Pocket, once ──► pocket.safetensors (shipped)
```

- The public Pocket refuses an audio file (`has_voice_cloning` is False) but loads a saved `.safetensors`
  state; tested 2026-10-26: no token, an empty HF cache, and output identical to the gated model's render
  from the clip (same seed, differences only at rounding level).
- `clip.wav` is the source of truth. `pocket.safetensors` is tied to the Pocket model it was made with (pin
  `pocket-tts==4.0.1` and the `english_2026-10` config: gated weights `db04f0e…`, public weights `a7056f5…`).
  A new Pocket model means re-exporting all ten.
- **A saved Hugging Face login loads the gated model** even when the public one is asked for (seen 2026-10-26
  on the Mac). Listeners have no login, but tools that must run as a listener does (`qa.py`, the engine's tests
  of a shipped coach) hide it: their own `HF_HOME`, `HF_HUB_DISABLE_IMPLICIT_TOKEN=1`, no `HF_TOKEN`.

## Each coach ships

```
coaches/coach-<name>-local/         # see decision D1 for where this lives
  persona.md          schema 6: name, description, [voice] (the design description), [design] (record), [local]
  clip.wav            the master: 24 kHz mono 16-bit, about 20 s at 150 to 175 wpm
  clip.txt            its exact words
  pocket.safetensors  Pocket's voice state, 8 to 13 MB (it grows with the clip)
  pace.json           Pocket's measured pace (the same on processor and card: same weights)
  sample.mp3          about 15 s on Pocket, for setup to play
```

## Decisions

- **D1. Where shipped coaches live: in the engine, `coaches/` at the repo root** (recommended), not copied
  into `me/personas/`. They're public, versioned assets: a release that improves a coach should reach every
  listener, and ten coaches (about 100 MB) don't belong in each listener's private backup. `me/personas/` keeps
  web coaches, archived coaches and per-computer state (this computer's render speed, seeded from the shipped
  `pace.json`; see `voices.seed_pace`). `personas.all()` merges the two places, a `me/` folder winning on a name
  clash. This reverses the 9.x-era move of Andy into `me.example/`, so record why in the changelog.
- **D2. Andy: redesigned** on VoxCPM2 from his description. Compare old and new by ear before replacing him.
  Keep `aliases = ["Coach Demo"]`.
- **D3. The take check's recognizer: Parakeet 0.6B v3 through ONNX everywhere,** retiring the MLX one
  (`voices.CHECK_MODEL`). Measure its speed on the PC's processor in Phase B; if it's too slow there, say so
  before choosing anything else (the 110M is not the answer: see above).
- **D4. Clip length: about 20 s, unless the length test says otherwise.** Pace is what's specified (150 to 175
  wpm in the clip). Whether a longer clip helps is open: step 3 tests it on Sam.

## Phase A: make the voices (maintainer only)

On the RTX 2080 Super PC (2026-10-26): Hugging Face login with accepted Pocket terms; `models/` holds
`openbmb/VoxCPM2` (rev `32279eff…`) and `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign`; environments
`.cache/design/envs/voxcpm/` (voxcpm 2.0.3, torch 2.8.0 cu128), `.cache/bench/envs/pocket-cuda/`, `pocket-cpu/`,
`turbo-cuda/` (for the speaker encoder only). All git-ignored. The Mac has `pocket-cpu` and `turbo-cpu`, enough
for QA but not for design.

Tools, in `tools/coachmaker/` (checked in, not part of any listener command):
- `design.py`: takes from a description (VoxCPM2 by default), each made into a clip candidate (24 kHz mono,
  ends trimmed, leveled), heard by the recognizer, and shown with its pace in `.cache/design/listen/index.html`
  (red outside 150 to 175 wpm). `--script long` uses `script_long`.
- `pick.py`: copies a chosen take to `coaches/`, writes `clip.txt` from that take's script, records the pick.
- `export_pocket.py`: the gated Pocket makes `pocket.safetensors` from `clip.wav`.
- `qa.py`: below.

Steps:
1. **Done:** the ten first clips and their voice files, and QA of all ten (above).
2. **Redesign the five fast coaches and Andy** on the PC:
   `.venv/Scripts/python tools/coachmaker/design.py andy marcus theo callum maya --takes 6`. The roster's pace
   hint now asks for a slow, clear voice; six takes give a few at a coaching pace. Pick by ear among the
   takes inside 150 to 175 wpm.
3. **The length test, on Sam:** `design.py sam --takes 6` and `design.py sam --takes 6 --script long`. Pick
   the best standard take and the best long one, export both, QA both. If the long clip is clearly better on
   short lines and WER, redesign every coach with `script_long`; if not, keep about 20 s.
4. **Pick, export, QA:** `pick.py <coach> <take>`, `export_pocket.py <coach>`, then
   `qa.py --device cuda` (or no flag on the Mac). Pass: no line failing all four takes, WER under 1.5%,
   Pocket at 150 to 195 wpm, Pocket's takes like the clip (0.80 or more), no two clips alike (under 0.85),
   at most two lines drifting from the clip. The similarity limits aren't calibrated yet: a coach close to
   another (Kate and Claire, 0.876) is checked by ear, not redesigned on the number alone. Then listen to
   `.cache/design/qa/index.html` end to end; the ear approves every coach.
5. **Samples**: render `sample.mp3` for each on Pocket.

## Phase B: the engine

Pocket as the engine:
- `pyproject.toml`: one local extra per platform holding `pocket-tts==4.0.1` (PyTorch, processor or CUDA) and
  the recognizer (`onnx-asr`, `onnxruntime`). Drop `mlx-audio`, `chatterbox-tts` and the qwen packages. Run
  `uv lock` and check every platform resolves.
- `tools/voices.py`: `ENGINES = ("pocket",)`; the public Pocket model in `LOCAL_MODELS` with its pinned
  revision (and the tokenizer repo its config names) and `SIZES_GB`; the ONNX recognizer as `CHECK_MODEL`.
  Drop `VOICE_DESIGN`, `DESIGN_LINE`, chatterbox and qwen3. Pocket loads its weights through `hf://` paths: check
  they land in `models/` (`paths.py` points `HF_HUB_CACHE` there), that every file `english_2026-10` names is
  downloaded at setup (the Mac's copy lacked some on 2026-10-26), and pass `language="english_2026-10"`
  explicitly, so a pocket-tts default change can't swap the model under the shipped voice files.
- `tools/engines.py`: `_pocket_speaker(voice)` loads `TTSModel.load_model(language=…)`, moves to CUDA when
  available, loads `pocket.safetensors` with `get_state_for_audio_prompt`, and speaks with
  `torch.manual_seed(seed)` then `generate_audio(state, text)`; `runtime()` reports `pocket-tts <v> torch <v>
  cpu|cuda`. Refuse to run if the gated model loaded (a saved login), with how to fix it. `listener()` is the
  ONNX Parakeet. Remove the MLX paths, chatterbox and `design_takes`.
- `tools/personas.py`: `LOCAL_ENGINES = ("pocket",)`; a `pocket_voice` property; `voice_for()` builds the
  Pocket voice dict.
- `tools/models.py`: `pinned()` holds Pocket and the recognizer.
- `tools/setup.sh` and `tools/doctor.*`: local voices run on every Mac and PC; setup downloads Pocket and the
  recognizer (about 0.5 GB and 2.5 GB; confirm). No "experimental on the processor" warning. The models for
  Turbo, Qwen3 and the designer can be removed from `models/` (`./sweatcast models` says which are unused).
- `tools/render.py`: `CHECK_TAKES` stays four (Pocket's seed and three more); a line whose four takes all drop
  a cue stops the build, as today. The take check runs on every computer now.

Removing local custom coaches:
- `tools/coaches.py`: remove `design`, `can_design`, `_design_takes`, the local `keep`, `set … local.engine`,
  and the local half of `download` (setup does it). Keep `list`, `last`, `measured`, `archive`, `set` for web
  settings, and every web command.
- `tools/personas.py`, `tools/check.py`, `me.example/.gitignore`: drop `takes/`.
- `PERSONA_SCHEMA = 6` in `tools/context.py`, with a step in `tools/migrate.py`: move every local persona in
  `me/personas/` that isn't one of the shipped ten into `personas/.archive/`, delete
  `me/personas/coach-andy-local/` (D1), and bump schema in the rest.
- `tools/bench.py` and `bench_worker.py` stay (a measurement tool; Turbo and Qwen3 stay candidates).

## Risks and checks

- **No fallback engine.** A line Pocket can't say in four takes stops the build. The one-word rule removes the
  cause seen so far; if builds still stop, look at the line first (wording), then the coach's clip.
- **Pocket "can't pause"** (2026-10-25 research): the renderer adds the gaps between clips; listen for rushed
  lines within a paragraph. Claire's first takes showed the opposite (long gaps), from a slow clip.
- **Voice files and Pocket upgrades**: pin `pocket-tts` and the language config; a test should fail if the pin
  moves without re-exported voice files.
- **Designer licenses**: VoxCPM2 is Apache 2.0 (Qwen3-TTS, used for the first Andy, too); the designed voices
  are original (no real person), which also satisfies Pocket's terms against cloning without consent.
- **Repository size**: about 100 MB for the ten coaches. If git history gets heavy, move the voice files to
  release assets that setup downloads. Remade clips add to history; keep re-picks few.

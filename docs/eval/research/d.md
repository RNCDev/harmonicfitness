## Gardening / myth_or_fact: Sand loosens clay soil

Sam gardens; this is advice that gets passed over a lot of fences.

If your garden soil is heavy, sticky clay, mixing in sand will loosen it up and help it drain. Myth or fact?

Answer: Myth, mostly. Unless you add a huge amount, more than the clay itself, the fine clay fills the gaps between the sand grains and you get something closer to concrete. What actually loosens clay is organic matter: compost and leaves, dug in or laid on top, year after year.

## Personal finance, explained plainly / explainer: The rule of 72

Sam wants money explained plainly; this is the handiest shortcut there is.

Want to know how long it takes money to double? Divide 72 by the yearly growth rate. At 6 percent a year, 72 divided by 6 is 12: about 12 years to double. At 8 percent, about 9 years. It's an estimate, but a good one, and you can do it in your head between sets.

It works backward on debt, too. A card charging 24 percent a year doubles what you owe in about 3 years if you pay nothing. That's the plain reason people say to pay off high-interest debt first: the same arithmetic that grows savings slowly grows card balances fast.

Source: https://example.org/rule-of-72 (2026-10-20)

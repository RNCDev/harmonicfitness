# Eval results: the session builder's grid, first judged run

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Catalog commit:** e670fa6
- **Methods in `knowledge/sessions.toml`:** straight-sets, paired-sets, circuit, intervals, tabata, bike-intervals, flow, skill. Chosen in this run: circuit (16 cases), straight-sets (10), paired-sets (10), intervals (2), flow (2). Tabata, bike-intervals and skill were never chosen.
- **How the run was done:** the forty cases in `docs/eval/cases.toml` were composed with `./sweatcast session`. Eight writers, five cases each, turned the skeletons into scripts (`docs/eval/out/<id>.draft.txt`), with no research and no audio: topic beats are placeholders, and each draft was only linted. Four judges, ten cases each, scored them on the same rubric (`docs/eval/judge.md`). This file merges their four parts and the writers' eight reports.
- **Caveat:** each judge scored only their own ten cases, so calibration may differ between judges (part means run from efficacy 2.7 for c01 to c10 down to 1.9 for c31 to c40).

## Five lowest cases

Ranked by the sum of the four scores, lower safety first on a tie. c38 and c12 tie exactly on every score (4, 2, 3, 3), so both are given as the fifth.

**1. c21 (10: safety 3, efficacy 2, adherence 2, ease 3). Listener c, advanced, general, 20 min, full gym, paired-sets.** The fault lies mostly in the skeleton. Every slot puts its bodyweight or novice candidate first (incline push-up ahead of dumbbell bench press, air squat as the only squat), and the writer took the first candidate, so a barbell lifter got incline push-ups and air squats at 3x8, two knee-dominant lower slots, no hinge and no row. The slot of 202 s for 3x8 leaves two rests of about 65 s, not the 120 s printed, yet the script says "about two minutes". Paired-sets gives each movement its own marker, so the pairs were run as straight sets. The case limit `calf=off-limits:left` never reached the Health lines, and no lunge or squat in the catalog rates a calf load, so reverse lunges ran on both sides with the left rear foot on its toes. The script's "keep the left heel down" cue for a reverse lunge contradicts the movement; that part is the script's fault.

**2. c24 (11: safety 3, efficacy 2, adherence 3, ease 3). Listener c, advanced, general, 30 min, bodyweight, paired-sets.** The fault lies in the skeleton and the catalog. With the low back off limits and only a mat, every bodyweight pull (`prone-y-t-w-raise`, low back 1) and hinge (`glute-bridge`, low back 1) is removed, leaving two pushes, a lunge and a squat: no pull at all. The case's `neck=delicate` limit isn't echoed in the skeleton, and `pike-push-up` passed on a neck rating of 1 although it is an inverted press with the head toward the floor. The writer had to invent a "gentle, head level" pike and a slow finisher. 4x6 bodyweight is no dose for an advanced lifter; the skeleton could have offered tempo or isometric variants.

**3. c33 (11: safety 5, efficacy 1, adherence 2, ease 3). Listener c, advanced, conditioning, 12 min, full gym, circuit.** The fault is in the skeleton. The method came from rotation ("circuit, because the last session was straight-sets"), which pulled the advanced strength dose (reps 4 to 8, rest 120 to 180 s) into a conditioning goal: three rounds of four incline push-ups, reverse lunges and air squats, about 36 easy reps and roughly eight minutes of standing still, with the rower and air bike unused. The ranking put the novice incline push-up ahead of the push-up, and the case's `neck=delicate` limit was missing from the Health lines, so the writer added it. The script gave the sided lunge "four reps" without saying per side and padded the rests with repeated filler.

**4. c34 (11: safety 5, efficacy 1, adherence 2, ease 3). Listener c, advanced, conditioning, 35 min, full gym, circuit.** The same shape at 35 minutes: 5x8 of incline push-up, reverse lunge, air squat and chest-supported row with 180 s of rest per round, so each 352 s round is about half rest. The rower, air bike, ski-erg and sled are never touched, because the circuit's patterns hold no locomotion and those entries are still drafts. The fault is in the skeleton (method, dose and level ranking); the script is safe for the off-limits low back but can only narrate rest.

**5 (tied). c38 (12: safety 4, efficacy 2, adherence 3, ease 3). Listener b, experienced, targeted (horizontal pull, back), 30 min, hotel gym, straight-sets.** The fault is mostly the skeleton. Straight-sets put the target in one slot (single-arm row, 3x8) and spent the rest on glute bridge, seated shoulder press and air squat (air squat chosen over goblet squat), so the target got about 4 of 13 sets counting the finisher, which the writer filled by reusing the row. The skeleton offered overhead pressing to a listener whose neck is delicate, though the playbook treats pain-free overhead work as the test for clearing a delicate neck. The warm-up's "let the head hang heavy" is the script's fault.

**5 (tied). c12 (12: safety 4, efficacy 2, adherence 3, ease 3). Listener c, advanced, strength, 40 min, hotel gym, paired-sets.** The fault is mostly the skeleton, plus the catalog. The first candidates were incline push-up, reverse lunge and air squat, with the dumbbell options last, so a lifter who does barbell work five days a week got 4x5 bodyweight work with two minutes of rest; only the pike push-up was hard enough. The case's `groin=delicate:left` limit never reached the skeleton, and `reverse-lunge` carries no groin rating, so it ran on both sides; the script passes only because the catalog can't see the risk. The cool-down used the unrated draft `standing-quad-stretch`.

## What this says about the catalog

The count in brackets is how many of the four judges raised the point; "writers" marks points raised in the writers' reports.

- **Groin is never rated** [3 judges; writers 3, 6, 8]. No entry has a `groin` key in `loads` or `support`, so a groin limit filters nothing (c12, c30, c39). Rate groin on `reverse-lunge`, `split-squat` and `bulgarian-split-squat` (1 to 2, on the working and support sides), on wide-stance squats, `lateral-shuffle`, `worlds-greatest-stretch`, wide-knee `childs-pose` and `figure-four-stretch` (at least 1, the lunges and wide stretches at 2). The ten entries that list `adductors` in `muscles` are a starting point.
- **Calf is rated only on the cardio moves** [2 judges; writers 2, 5, 6]. Only `jumping-jacks`, `high-knees`, `treadmill-incline-walk` and `treadmill-run` rate the calf, so `calf=delicate:right` (c06), `calf=off-limits:left` (c21) and `calf=delicate:both` (c27) removed nothing. Add `support = { calf = 1 }` or 2 (rear foot on its toes) to `split-squat`, `reverse-lunge` and `bulgarian-split-squat`, calf 1 to 2 in `loads` on `step-up`, and look again at `air-squat`. Finish `seated-calf-raise` (a draft) and add a standing calf raise.
- **A low back off limits leaves no loaded lower body** [3 judges]. Every hinge and loaded squat rates the low back (`glute-bridge` 1, `hip-thrust` 1, `romanian-deadlift` 2, `goblet-squat` 1, `leg-press` 2), so advanced listener c falls back to `air-squat`, `box-squat` and `wall-sit` (c09, c10, c11, c12, c21, c22). Finish `leg-extension`; add a seated or lying leg curl, hip abduction and adduction machines, a hack or belt squat, and loaded lunge variants (dumbbell split squat, dumbbell reverse lunge, dumbbell step-up) with honest low-back and wrist ratings. `bulgarian-split-squat` and `step-up` don't say whether they're loaded, so the dose can't ask for weight.
- **A low back off limits leaves bodyweight kits with no pull, hinge or core** [1 judge]. `prone-y-t-w-raise`, `glute-bridge`, `dead-bug`, `bird-dog` and `forearm-plank` all rate low back 1 (c23, c24). Add a pull or anti-extension entry rated low back 0 (a supported band pull-apart, `pallof-press` if it rates 0, side-lying external rotation), or reconsider `dead-bug` at 1 given the playbook keeps neutral-spine core work going.
- **Neck ratings are thin** [2 judges]. `pike-push-up` (`neck = 1`) is an inverted press with the head toward the floor and should be 2, which would keep it out under a delicate neck (c24). `glute-bridge` and `prone-y-t-w-raise` carry no neck load though one flexes the neck under load and the other holds the head up; rate both `neck = 1` (c15, c19, c20).
- **Trunk loads may be underrated** [1 judge; writers 3, 7]. `air-squat`, `box-squat`, `reverse-lunge` and `bulgarian-split-squat` rate low back 0 while `goblet-squat` is 1. Under the file's own "when unsure, rate up" rule, low back 1 on bodyweight squats and lunges is defensible; review it (c33, c34).
- **`incline-push-up` has `needs = []` but every cue puts the hands on a bench** [2 judges; writer 3]. Writers invented a bench, table, couch or desk (c01, c02, c13). Give it `needs = ["bench|chair"]` (or a wall) and add a wall push-up, or make its setup a wall or counter.
- **Drafts hide whole kits** [3 judges]. Conditioning machines (`rower-intervals`, `air-bike-sprint`, `air-bike-steady`, `ski-erg`, `sled-push`, `sled-pull`, `stair-climber`, `bike-standing-sprint`, `bike-standing-climb`) have no level and are never planned, so the full gym has no hard conditioning (c33, c34) [2]. The warm-up and cool-down moves every writer used (`arm-circles`, `shoulder-rolls`, `leg-swings`, `standing-quad-stretch`, `cross-body-shoulder-stretch`, `standing-calf-stretch`) are drafts with no loads, so no limit screens them (c12's quad stretch under a delicate groin) [1]. `towel-hamstring-curl` and `bench-dip` are drafts too [1].
- **Too few harder moves for an experienced listener in a hotel room** [1 judge]. `push-up` is the only experienced horizontal push, and nothing in the chair-and-towel kit is harder than `single-leg-glute-bridge` or `bulgarian-split-squat`. Add a decline push-up (chair) and a feet-elevated single-leg bridge (chair), and finish the drafts above.
- **Interval entries carry no effort** [1 judge]. Interval slots offer `marching-in-place`, `bike-easy-spin` and `treadmill-walk` ahead of `bike-cadence-surge`, `bike-seated-sprint` and `treadmill-run`, so the "hard efforts" in c31 and c32 were an easy spin. Add an effort field (easy, moderate, hard): work phases take hard entries, warm-ups easy ones; never offer `bike-easy-spin`, `bike-steady` or `marching-in-place` as interval work above novice.
- **`spine-flexion` and `spine-twist` never filter** [1 judge]. `childs-pose` and `thread-the-needle` carry them, but no rule acts on them when the low back is off limits (c39). Make both excluded flags under an off-limits low back.
- **`towel-door-row`'s setup is easy to misread** [1 judge]. "Only on a solid door closed toward you" became "closes toward you" and "opens away from you" (c15, c16, c18). Spell out the hinge side.

## What this says about the dose rules

- **Bodyweight entries get loaded-lift reps** [4 judges]. `dose.strength.experienced` (6 to 10) and `advanced` (4 to 8) are applied to `air-squat`, `incline-push-up`, `glute-bridge` and `reverse-lunge`: 3x7 (c08), 4x7 (c10), 4x6 (c05, c17), 3x6 (c11, c18), 4x5 (c12), 3x8 (c21), with 90 to 120 s rests. For an entry with no load in `needs`, use a bodyweight band (about 12 to 20 at experienced, 15 to 25 at advanced), a slow tempo (`seconds_per_rep = 4`) or a 40 s hold, or require a loaded or harder variant above novice.
- **Circuits use the strength rest** [4 judges]. "Rest 60/90/120/180 s" per slot reads as rest after every station, against the method's "little rest" (c11 120 s, c19 90 s, c23 120 s, c29 105 s, c34 180 s), and writers padded between stations with rest talk. Give circuits 15 to 30 s of transition between stations and put `rest_seconds` (about 60 to 90 s) between rounds only, and print it that way.
- **Printed rests don't match the slot seconds** [3 judges; writers 1, 3]. Slots are sized as if the rest overlapped a partner's work: c03's 112 s for 2x12 plus 60 s, c15's 127 s for 2x12 plus 90 s, c12's 250 s for 4x5 plus 3x120 s, c17's 217 s for 4x6 plus 3x90 s, c21's 202 s leaving 65 s against a printed 120 s, c25's 93 s leaving 40 s against 60 s. Print the rest the slot actually holds, or size the slot from the printed rest, and say where the rest falls (between sets, between rounds, or covered by the partner).
- **Warm-up, finisher and cool-down get seconds but no content** [3 judges; all eight writers]. Writers invented the same marching-with-punches or reused squats and push-ups (c02, c04, c06, c08, c10, c14, c16, c18, c20, c22, c24, c38, c40), two of them easy marching with no training effect (c18, c20). Give the finisher two or three kit-filtered candidates and a conditioning dose (for example 40/20 x 3 to 4), give the warm-up and cool-down a short candidate list, or drop the finisher under 30 minutes.
- **`hard_sets_per_session` is never enforced** [2 judges]. c02 has 18 sets (novice cap 12), c03 and c04 14, c26 21 and c28 27 (novice [6, 12]), c06 18 (experienced cap 16), c10 and c22 24 (advanced [12, 20]). The composer adds slots until the time is used. Cap slots or sets per slot at the maximum and give leftover time to the finisher or longer rests.
- **Conditioning gets the strength dose** [2 judges]. Every conditioning circuit (c25 to c30, c33 to c36) ran `intensity = "strength"` reps with 60 to 180 s rests; `[dose.conditioning.*]` was never used. A conditioning circuit should run timed work at the conditioning dose (30 to 60 s per station at advanced).
- **Sided reps and holds are ambiguous** [2 judges]. "2 sets of 12" became twelve alternating reps on `reverse-lunge` (c01 to c04) and six per arm on `single-arm-row` (c04), but "9 each side" in c06 and c08; c33 gave "four reps" on a lunge; "2 rounds of 30 s" on a sided stretch became one hold per side (c37). Say "per side" for `sided = true` entries and size the slot for both sides.
- **General uses the strength dose** [1 judge]. Novice general at 30 minutes with 2x12 and 90 s rest (c14) is 12 easy sets and much rest; use 60 s rest or three sets.
- **Rest is too long in short sessions** [1 judge]. Advanced rests of 120 to 180 s eat a 15-minute session (c11). Cap rest under 20 minutes.
- **Rounds are uneven** [1 judge; writers 3, 6, 8]. The last round runs about half the others (c23 126 s against 260 s, c29 124 s against 236 s, c11 135 s), so writers cut setups. Split the block evenly across rounds.
- **Interval rounds multiply by slots** [1 judge]. Rounds of 6 to 8 per slot times five slots gave 30 efforts at 45 minutes (c32) and 14 at 20 (c31). Cap total efforts at about 10 to 16, give leftover time to a steady block, and budget 60 to 90 s whenever a slot changes machine (floor to bike in c31; bike to treadmill in c32) [writer 7].
- **Flow gives breathing as many slots as mobility** [1 judge]. c37 spent 350 of 900 s and c39 490 of 1350 s breathing. Cap breathing at one slot in the block and leave the rest to the cool-down.
- **Targeted sessions barely weight the target** [1 judge]. 3 of 12 sets in c38, 3 of 9 in c40. Give the target at least half the hard sets: two slots, or more sets in its slot.

## What this says about the methods

- **paired-sets can't be delivered as pairs** [3 judges; writers 1 to 5]. `--draft` emits one marker per movement, so every paired case (c03, c05, c09, c10, c12, c15, c17, c21, c22, c24) was written as straight sets, an odd slot count leaves one movement unpaired (c03's dead bug), and the time saving is lost. Emit one marker per pair ("Pair 1: squat + horizontal push, set 1 of 4") with A1/A2 alternation and the rest counted across the partner's set, or stop choosing paired-sets (or time its slots as straight sets) until then.
- **Rotation overrides fit** [3 judges]. Methods were chosen "because the last session was" something else: c05, c06, c09, c10, c11 (a 15-minute advanced strength session as a 6-rep bodyweight circuit), c20 (a 30-minute general session that dropped three patterns), c33, c34. Let variety break ties only among methods that fit the goal and length, and prefer methods whose `work` matches the goal's dose.
- **Circuit always wins conditioning** [2 judges]. `choose_method` takes the first fitting method in file order, and `circuit` lists `conditioning` and comes before `intervals`, `tabata` and `bike-intervals`, so every conditioning case with no log got a circuit (c25 to c30), even in the hotel gym where `bike-intervals` fits (c27, c28) and at 10 to 30 minutes for advanced listeners where Tabata fits (c33, c35, c36). Drop `conditioning` from `circuit.goals`, put the conditioning methods first, or rank by goal fit before file order; intervals on a finished `air-bike-sprint` or `rower-intervals` would suit c34.
- **paired-sets should keep a pull for each push** [1 judge]. Its `about` says "a push with a pull or an upper with a lower", yet c05 dropped horizontal pull and kept two pushes. When time drops slots, keep at least one pull per push.
- **straight-sets fits poorly at 30 to 45 minutes** [2 judges]. It fills six slots at three or more sets and overshoots the caps (c02, c06), or fits only four slots and drops patterns (c20 lost both horizontals and the lunge; c14 fit six only at two sets). Use fewer, harder movements at the long end; for general at 20 to 30 minutes prefer circuit or a working paired-sets.
- **Short or bodyweight-only sessions above novice need timed or denser work** [2 judges]. At 15 minutes or less a circuit should run timed stations (EMOM, or 40 s on and 20 s off) rather than 3 rounds of 8 with 120 s rests (c23); in a chair-and-towel hotel room, experienced and advanced listeners need tempo, unilateral, or density/EMOM sets rather than 4x6 air squats (c17, c18).
- **circuit works for a novice at 15 minutes** [2 judges]. c01, c07 and c13 (the best of c11 to c20) map cleanly onto the round markers; at experienced and advanced (c11, c19) it fails only on dose and candidate order.
- **Intervals scale poorly past about 25 minutes** [1 judge]. One pattern in short bouts became 30 identical segments at 45 minutes (c32). Mix in a steady or tempo block, or let `bike-intervals` take one block.
- **Targeted straight-sets follows the generic order** [1 judge]. It fills squat, hinge, push, pull and drops patterns "for time": c40 (glutes) kept the push-up and dropped the lunge and single-leg bridge; c38 (back) spent slots on hinge, vertical push and squat. Put the target pattern first, repeat it, and fill from patterns sharing its muscles.
- **Flow suits novice mobility but runs out at 30 minutes** [1 judge]. c37 works and needs only the breathing cap and the sided-hold fix; c39 repeated thread the needle and never used the full gym's `foam-roller` or `strap` entries. Widen the candidate pool by equipment, or end the flow sooner with longer holds.

## What this says about the composer

- **Candidates are ranked by file order, not level or kit** [4 judges; nearly every writer took the first candidate]. `air-squat`, `incline-push-up` and `glute-bridge` (novice, `needs = []`) come first for experienced and advanced listeners with dumbbells or a full gym, ahead of `goblet-squat`, `dumbbell-bench-press`, `floor-press`, `hip-thrust`, `push-up` and `bulgarian-split-squat` (c05, c06, c08, c09, c10, c11, c12, c19, c20, c21, c22, c33, c34, c38). Rank by closeness of `level` to the listener's, then by use of the kit's load (entries whose `needs` the kit meets over `needs = []`), or follow each entry's `harder` chain above novice.
- **A case `--limit` never reaches the Health lines** [3 judges; writers 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]. c06, c09, c12, c15, c21, c24, c27, c30, c33 and c39 show only health.md's rows, so writers spoke the limit from `cases.toml`. Print the `--limit` as a Health line like the profile's limits, and print "healthy side only" on sided slots when a sided limit applies.
- **Slot dropping for time loses balance and liked moves** [3 judges, in their notes]. Dropped: horizontal pull with two pushes kept (c05), lunge and core (c07), vertical push and core (c08), horizontal pull and vertical push (c09), lunge, vertical push and the liked dead bug (c17), the liked dead bug for a pike push-up under a delicate low back (c18), both horizontals and the lunge (c20), lunge and single-leg bridge in a glute session (c40). Keep push/pull balance, the target, and the listener's liked movements when dropping.
- **Rest-heavy, single-movement segments force filler** [4 judges in adherence scores; writers 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8]. Segments of 150 to 370 s for one movement needed about three words a second, more than the filler pool holds, so generic lines repeat within a file (c02's 346 s lunge, c16, c17, c18, c31, c32). - **Script-side habits the skill should stop** [1 judge]. Set calls of about five spoken seconds ("At the bell, go... Halfway... Rack it. Rest.") for a 36 s set, "rack it" after bodyweight sets, and rest talk inside little-rest circuits (c02 to c04, c07). The script should cue during the work and say "rack it" only with weights. Warm-ups also carry the opening safety text, so a short warm-up (c19, 135 s) is mostly rules [writer 4].
- **Lint and eval harness** [writers only]. The brief's lint command had no build name, so `--lint-only` was taken as the name and real renders started (all eight writers); the command must be `./sweatcast build <draft> "eval-<id>" --lint-only --allow-estimated`. Lint-only still exports a `.txt` into `<listener>/workouts/` and exits 1 if that folder is missing [writers 2, 7]. The locator check accepts only words like block, round, set and minute, so pairs needed "Block N of M" [writer 1].

@@ 70 | Welcome
Thirty minutes, a chair and the floor, and every set built to make you stronger. I'm Desmond. We go slow today. Three seconds down on every rep, up in one, because slow is what makes your own bodyweight heavy.

Quick truth first. This one's ready-made. An AI wrote it once, for whoever presses play, so it wasn't made for you today, and it doesn't know your injuries. It's not a certified trainer or a clinician talking either. Your body and your doctor outrank me.

The rules. Effort's the job, and an ache of two or three out of ten is fine. Skip or ease off anything that hurts. Sharp pain or new swelling, stop that move.

Numbness, tingling, dizziness, or a joint giving way, you're done for today. Chest pain or pressure, feeling faint, or a pounding, racing heart, stop everything and get help.

Kit's simple. A sturdy chair with its back to a wall, a towel, and a bit of floor. There's an easier and a harder way for everything, and you'll hear both. Up you get.

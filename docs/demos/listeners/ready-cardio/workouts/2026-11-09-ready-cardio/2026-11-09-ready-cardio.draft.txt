@@ 66 | Welcome
Thirty minutes of cardio, no jumping, no impact, so it suits any floor and whoever lives below you. I'm Desmond. Quick heads-up first. This is a ready-made session, written by AI and the same for everyone. It wasn't made for you, and no certified trainer or clinician checked it against your body, so you and your doctor make the calls.

It isn't adapted to any injury either, so skip or ease off anything that hurts. Here's where the line sits. A mild ache, up to a two or three out of ten, is fine. Sharp pain or new swelling, stop that move.

Numbness, tingling, dizziness, or a joint that gives way, and you're done for today. Chest pain or pressure, feeling faint, or your heart racing and pounding, and you stop everything and get help.

Kit check. A sturdy chair with its back to a wall, a towel, and room to take a big step in any direction. Every move comes with an easier way and a harder way. On your feet, and we'll get moving.

@@ 98 | Warm-up, marching in place
Marching on the spot. Feet under your hips, arms loose. Knee up, then the other one, opposite arm swinging, and every foot lands soft and quiet. [count 3 go]
Nice and relaxed. At this pace, breathing through your nose should be no trouble.

Here's the plan. First we learn four moves, slowly. Then three rounds of those four. Each one is thirty seconds of hard work, then about a minute of marching to get your breath back.

Hard means about a seven out of ten. Zero is lying on the couch, and ten is sprinting for a bus that's pulling away. At a seven, you can get out a few words at a time, but not a sentence. The marching in between is a three or four, where you could chat.

If the march feels easy, lift the knees higher and pump the arms. If it doesn't, just ease it down.

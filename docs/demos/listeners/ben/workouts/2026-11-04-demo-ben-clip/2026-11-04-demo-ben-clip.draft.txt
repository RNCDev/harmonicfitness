@@ 75 | Box squat, set one
Right, Ben, here's your first real movement, and you already do it every day: sitting down in a chair and standing back up. It's called a box squat, and the chair is the box.

Step one, your feet. Stand with your back to the chair, close enough to feel the seat on the backs of your legs, then take a small step forward, feet about hip width apart.

Step two, your hands. Cross them over your chest.

Step three, the move. Push your hips back, like you're shutting a car door with your bottom, and sit down slowly until you just tap the seat. Don't flop. Then stand up tall and squeeze. You'll feel it in your thighs and your glutes, your bottom muscles, not your lower back.

Ten, slow and steady, with a few left in the tank. [count 3 go] [reps 10]
Hips back, and tap the seat. [hold 3s]
Stand up tall, and squeeze. [hold 3s]
Chest stays proud, and push the floor away. [hold 3s]
Halfway, and nice and slow on the way down. [hold 2s]
Last one, all the way up, and rest.

Shake your legs out, and let your breathing settle. [pause 3s]

That's your first set, ten squats in the bank. If you could have done a few more, next round try a hover. Same squat, but you stop just above the seat, so the chair's only there to catch you. If those ten were hard work, do eight next round instead. Not sure? Try the hover.

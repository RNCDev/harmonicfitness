@@ 48 | Rest, the answer
Bell down, and breathe. [pause 4s] Got a number?

[story] Sixty-three matches. The trick is to count the losers, not the games. Every match sends exactly one team home, and for one champion to be left standing, sixty-three teams have to go home. [/story]

[story] You can add it up the long way if you like, thirty-two, sixteen, eight, four, two and one, and you still land on sixty-three. And it works for any cup. One match fewer than there are teams, so ten teams, nine matches. [/story]

I like that one. No adding up, just one good idea, and if you had it before that set was done, that's a sharp head for before work. Last round of squats next, the twenty-four, when you're ready.

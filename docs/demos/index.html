Demo clips

Seven clips of about two minutes, so someone can hear what HarmonicFitness does before they set anything up. Each is the chosen segments of a full workout of twenty-five to forty-five minutes, written by the engine for an invented listener from one ask in their own words, and spoken by an ElevenLabs coach.

Built 2026-11-04 · on the bucket · on the sign-in page since 0.65.0

The clips and demos.json are at https://harmonicfitness-media.fly.storage.tigris.dev/demos/v1/, publicly readable.

Three coaches, each one of ElevenLabs's own stock voices, chosen by ear:

  • Priya, the voice Lily (British, warm; a physio who coaches): the three limits, Jo, Ines and Frank.
  • Desmond, the voice Charlie (Australian, deep; driving): the strength sessions, Lena and Mo.
  • Bea, the voice Matilda (American, warm; patient, a storyteller): the beginner and the stories, Ben and Ravi.

All three speak with eleven_v4, ElevenLabs's more natural model, never the turbo one. A stock voice has the same ID on every account and takes no voice slot, so nothing is designed. Designed voices were tried first and turned down by ear, tinny and breathy, even on ElevenLabs's newer design model.

How a clip is made

The whole workout is written and linted, which costs nothing. Only the clip's segments are rendered: about ten thousand characters for the seven, around eighty cents on eleven_v4, instead of a hundred and seventy thousand for the full workouts.

  1. Write the whole workout.

    The workout skill, as that listener, from the brief. The writer arranges it so the clip's segments ([demo.clip] segments) fall together, add up to about two minutes and make sense heard alone.

  2. Lint it clean.

    Every check a listener's workout gets: the menu, the timing, the silences, what a voice can read.

  3. Cut the clip's segments.

    demos excerpt writes them, word for word, as a draft of their own, with the full draft's menu beside it. Under a one-sided limit the clip starts at the opening, because the build checks that the first segment names the side.

  4. Render only the clip.

    A normal build of the clip's draft: linted, gated by the menu, spoken by the coach. Its card says “two minutes from a thirty-minute session”, which is true: the script is a full session that passed every check.

The privacy hook's paths are anchored at the repo's root, so it spares these files, as it spares docs/x/log.md in its test.

The listeners are built so the clips stay right for a year:

  • No feeds, and every news format in skip_formats: only evergreen stories.
  • “Never bring up” holds the date, the day, the season and the weather, so research and the writer leave them out.
  • An empty log, kept empty (no log-session for a demo), so no demo refers to a history it doesn't have.
  • writing_rules in demos.toml, given to the writer with every demo: no dates, every fact checked against two sources.

Before building

  1. A paid ElevenLabs plan.

    The home page is public, so the plan's terms must cover commercial use of the audio. Stock voices take no voice slot.

  2. The key in me/.env.

    Nothing to copy. demos run points the engine at it through HARMONICFITNESS_KEYS, so a demo listener's folder needs no key file of its own and only the engine reads the key. ./harmonicfitness keys shows ElevenLabs set.

  3. About ten thousand characters, plus a 45-second sample a demo (about a cent each). The skill says what it will spend and waits for a yes before anything is billed.
  4. The Fly bucket the clips are stored in, named in demos.toml (bucket).

Build

Ask Claude Code (or Codex) to “build the demos”: the demos skill runs these, demo by demo. Every command for a demo listener goes through demos run, which sets its folder and the key file for that one command.

  1. The listener's folder.

    docs/demos/listeners/<slug>/ is the demo's own context folder, never me/, which is the real listener's. demos home installs its coach there from docs/demos/coaches/, held to eleven_v4, and the coach's sample records its pace, for the lint. What the engine generates in the folder is git-ignored.

    ./harmonicfitness demos home jo
./harmonicfitness demos run jo coaches sample "Coach Priya"
  2. The whole workout, written and linted. 
    ./harmonicfitness demos run jo brief --goal general --minutes 30 --preset hotel-room --coach "Coach Priya" \
  --save docs/demos/listeners/jo/workouts/<stem>/<stem>.draft.txt
./harmonicfitness demos run jo build "<draft>" "Demo jo" --lint-only --coach "Coach Priya"
  3. The clip, cut and rendered. 
    ./harmonicfitness demos excerpt "<draft>"                 # the segments, numbered
./harmonicfitness demos excerpt "<draft>" --from 1 --to 2
./harmonicfitness demos run jo build "<clip draft>" "Demo jo v1" --coach "Coach Priya"
  4. Listened to, published, recorded.

    If it falls flat, rewrite those segments, lint, cut and build again: only changed paragraphs are billed again. Then from, to, moments and changed go into demos.toml.

    ./harmonicfitness demos publish jo "<clip MP3>"     # to docs/demos/clips/jo.mp3, then the bucket
./harmonicfitness demos upload                         # clips and demos.json to the bucket
./harmonicfitness demos                                # how far each has got

The home page

Built in 0.65.0: the sign-in page's wide column, the form beside it (web/client/src/zones/Demos.tsx).

  • One ask at a time, as the app's own request. Each demo reads as the two lines a listener sends ("Hey, coach Priya, I'd like a 30-minute full-body workout in a hotel room, right now. Go easy on my left knee today."), the blanks in cream, from [demo.request]. Above it, who asked and what they're into. Arrows and dots move through the seven; it turns every ten seconds until someone plays, taps or tabs in, and never for anyone who asks for less motion.
  • Two minutes to play, straight from the bucket, with the moments marked on the line.
  • Behind one tap: their ask in their own words, who they are, their kit and injury, what changed for them, and the moments, each jumping the clip to it.
  • The data: ./harmonicfitness demos web writes web/client/src/demos.json from demos.toml (the cards, the requests, each clip's address), and the client builds with it. A test fails when it's out of step, and another when a request uses words the app's own lists don't offer.

Re-making them in a year

  1. Raise version in demos.toml, and clear from, to, moments and changed.
  2. Read the asks, the clips and the writing rules again.

    Change any that no longer show something new, and check model against knowledge/services.toml.

  3. Keep the three coaches.

    Their stock voice IDs are in docs/demos/coaches/, the same on any account. If ElevenLabs retires one, have a few stock voices speak the same coaching line on eleven_v4 and choose by ear.

  4. Ask to “re-make the demos”.

    Each demo's last scripts are kept in its workouts/, a starting point for the next ones.

  5. Run ./harmonicfitness demos web and ship the engine: the sign-in page builds with its demos.json, pointing at the new version's clips.

# Share HarmonicFitness

A message ready to send to a friend, by text, email or chat. Their AI assistant reads the link, downloads HarmonicFitness and sets them up.

## The message

Copy and send:

> I've been using HarmonicFitness, a workout podcast made fresh for me each time: it asks how I'm feeling, plans around my equipment and any injuries, and turns the session into an MP3 with a coach's voice and stories I care about. To try it, open the Claude desktop app (the **Code** tab; needs a paid plan) or the ChatGPT desktop app (**Codex**; any plan), open your home folder, and paste: *Can you use https://github.com/RNCDev/harmonicfitness and set me up?* It walks you through the rest.

## A shorter one, for a text

> Try HarmonicFitness, a workout podcast made fresh for you each time. On a computer, open the Claude desktop app's Code tab or ChatGPT's Codex, open your home folder, and paste: Can you use https://github.com/RNCDev/harmonicfitness and set me up?

## If they use Antigravity

Google's Antigravity should work too, but it hasn't been tried end to end yet. The same line works there: open the home folder as the workspace and paste *Can you use https://github.com/RNCDev/harmonicfitness and set me up?* Let them know it's the newest route.

## What they'll need

- **A Mac or a Windows PC.** Setup runs on their own computer, not on a phone or in a plain chat.
- **The voice, either way:**
  - **Free, on their computer:** on an Apple Silicon Mac or a Windows or Linux PC, after a one-time download of about 4 GB.
  - **A web coach, on any computer:** a pay-as-you-go key from a voice service, about $0.30 a workout on Fish Audio. Setup walks them through it, and the key goes from their clipboard straight into a private file, never into the chat.
- **A few steps:** four questions, picking a coach by ear, then a ten-minute first workout (on the free route, after the download).

Everything else is in the [README](README.md): where it runs, costs, and what happens to their files.

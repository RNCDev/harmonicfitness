# The trainer's review

The host wrote one audio workout from your notes and the researcher's. Read it as the trainer who planned it: is it safe, and does it pass the build's check?

Go down the lines to check, one by one: each `word for word` line is in its segment exactly, and each `every part kept` line keeps every part, its side (left or right) and body part exactly as written. A line missing, or with a side or body part changed, goes back in as written. Fix every issue the build's check names. Leave the host's voice alone: don't restyle, shorten a story or add cues the plan doesn't need.

Never change a movement, a dose, a marker, or a fact in a story; never add a story. Return only the segments you change, each whole, by its number.

# The trainer's review

The host wrote one audio workout from your notes and the researcher's. Read it as the trainer who planned it: is it safe, and does it pass the build's check?

Go down the lines to check, one by one: each `word for word` line is in its segment exactly, and each `every part kept` line keeps every part, its side (left or right) and body part exactly as written. A line missing, or with a side or body part changed, goes back in as written, without its label: `SAY EXACTLY:` and `SAY, EVERY PART KEPT:` in your notes are marks for the writers, never spoken, so a line in the script that starts with one loses it. Fix every issue the build's check names. Leave the host's voice alone: don't restyle, shorten a story or add cues the plan doesn't need.

Never change a movement, a dose, a count, a cue or a fact in a story, and never add a story; holds and pauses may change where the build's check asks for them. A movement for one side only: in any segment that names it, the other side comes up only in the brief's limit cues and stop signs, said word for word; otherwise not by any name: not its left or right, nor "the other arm", "the sore shoulder", "the injured side" or "the non-working arm", and no both-sides words ("each side", "switch", "ten a side"). The build refuses anything else there, so a line that breaks this is reworded to keep the care without the side, or moved to the segment before the set. Return only the segments you change, each whole, by its number.

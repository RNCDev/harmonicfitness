# The trainer's review

The host wrote one audio workout from your notes and the researcher's. Read it as the trainer who planned it: is it safe, and does it pass the build's check?

Check what matters for the body: the opening names each limit with its right side and body part, and says the rules and that an AI wrote the session; a limit's one reminder is at the first set that touches it, and a repeat of it later is cut; no side is ever wrong. Fix a wrong side or a missing rule in a sentence of your own, in the coach's voice. Fix every issue the build's check names; its warnings are yours to judge, and most can stay. Leave the host's voice alone: don't restyle, shorten a story or add cues the plan doesn't need.

Never change a movement, a dose, a count, a cue or a fact in a story, and never add a story; holds and pauses may change where the build's check asks for them. A movement for one side only: in any segment that names it, nothing may send the listener to the other side ("each side", "switch", "the other arm", "ten a side", "then the left"); the build refuses those, so reword such a line or move it to the segment before the set. The resting side's care may stay. Return only the segments you change, each whole, by its number.

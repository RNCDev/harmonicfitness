# Pulls and presses for small kits

Covers the evidence behind four movements added to fill the catalog's thinnest patterns for small kits (bodyweight, bands, one kettlebell or dumbbell without a bench): `band-lat-pulldown`, `band-overhead-press`, `staggered-stance-row` and `reverse-snow-angel`. It doesn't cover dosing, or the pull and push entries already in the catalog (see `2026-10-30-movement-library.md`).

Researched on 2026-11-08. Few sources here are clinical; most are exercise libraries, and the loads follow the catalog's own rules, rated up where unsure.

## Sources opened
- Tall Kneeling Lat Pull Down (Band), The Prehab Guys, https://theprehabguys.com/vimeo-video/tall-kneeling-lat-pull-down-band/ — band-lat-pulldown: band anchored very high in front, tall kneeling with hips fully extended, both hands palms down; glutes and core on, shoulder blades back, pull toward the pockets, don't shrug.
- Lat pull down with resistance band, Physitrack, https://ie.physitrack.com/home-exercise-video/lat-pull-down-with-resistance-band — band-lat-pulldown: band attached above head height over a door or other secure object, elbows bent, squeeze the shoulder blades, slow controlled return.
- Resistance Band Standing Shoulder / Overhead Press, WorkoutLabs, https://workoutlabs.com/exercise-guide/resistance-band-standing-shoulder-press-overhead-press/ — band-overhead-press: one foot on the middle of the band, the other stepped forward, band behind the arms; brace and don't let the back arch; press slowly overhead without locking the elbows.
- Reverse Snow Angel, The Prehab Guys, https://theprehabguys.com/vimeo-video/reverse-snow-angel/ — reverse-snow-angel: face down, head resting on a towel; hands as far from the floor as possible, slow and controlled, core and glutes on; faults are the low back arching with the head lifting, and shrugging.
- Dumbbell staggered stance row, trainwell, https://www.trainwell.net/exercises/dumbbell-staggered-stance-row — staggered-stance-row: the foot opposite the working hand forward, hinge with the back straight, elbow close to the body, lower under control, switch feet with hands.
- One-Arm Dumbbell Row guide, Gravitus, https://gravitus.com/guides/exercises/one-arm-dumbbell-row — staggered-stance-row: the no-bench version with the free hand on the thigh, back flat, elbow to the hip; the lower back holds the hinge with nothing under it (sets low-back = 2); faults: twisting open, curling the weight, shrugging, standing too upright. Tags the one-arm row as beginner.
- Bent over row, single arm with dumbbell, Physitrack, https://ie.physitrack.com/home-exercise-video/bent-over-row---single-arm-with-dumbbell — staggered-stance-row: the free hand supports on a waist-high object if needed; shoulder blades back and down, pull to the side of the ribs.

## Findings that set loads, levels or coaching
- band-lat-pulldown: kneeling, so knee = 2 and the kneeling and floor flags; arms overhead, so overhead; novice, as the catalog's level rule already places the pulldown. The anchor follows `band-row`: a shut door that opens away from you, so the pull presses it into its frame.
- band-overhead-press: split stance and no arching (WorkoutLabs); loads copied from `overhead-press` (low-back = 2, neck = 2) because a band press arches the same way, rated up. Novice, since a band starts light. A band slipping from under the foot is the first fault, as it snaps up toward the face.
- staggered-stance-row: unsupported hinge, so low-back = 2 (Gravitus), novice like `single-arm-row`, with that bench-supported row as its easier version and `bent-over-row` as its harder one. The hand on the thigh is the support side (wrist = 1, shoulder = 1, knee = 1).
- reverse-snow-angel: loads match `prone-y-t-w-raise` (shoulder = 2, low-back = 1, neck = 1); four seconds a rep for the slow sweep.

## Left out
- A doorframe row (gripping a door frame and leaning back) was researched and not added: the sources warn against loading door frames and handles, and with no one watching a slipped grip is a fall backward. `towel-door-row` stays the one door pull, with its latched-door setup.

## Not confirmed
- No source rates joint loads for these four on the catalog's scale; all four ratings are house judgment under the catalog's rules.
- No source gives a level for the band overhead press or the staggered-stance row; novice is house judgment.
